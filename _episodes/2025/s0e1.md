In this heartwarming and funny episode of PoducateMe, Hannah chats with her co-host Helen about Helen’s inspiring English learning journey—from her first awkward conversation at a Canadian airport to becoming a confident English teacher with over a decade of experience. Helen shares charming stories about learning English as a child in Ukraine, struggling through university-level linguistics, and adjusting to life in Canada—where even simple words like “Q-tip” and “hair elastic” threw her off. The episode highlights the ups and downs of language learning, the importance of a great teacher, and why confidence and persistence matter more than perfection. It wraps up with a light-hearted quick-fire round and advice every English learner will relate to.
