In this warm and enthusiastic kickoff episode, co-hosts Helen and Hannah, two experienced ESL educators, introduce their brand-new podcast, Paducate Me. They share the story behind the show’s creation—which, funnily enough, started with their off-topic chats about quirky English expressions and a shared love of linguistics.

Expect a podcast that’s not only about learning English, but doing it in a way that’s fun, meaningful, and real. Each week, you’ll get two episodes:

A conversation episode where they (and sometimes guests!) talk about everyday topics—work, hobbies, culture, and more.

A follow-up “Language Lab” episode that breaks down vocabulary and expressions from that conversation in a relaxed, practical way.

There’s no grammar stress, no boring lectures—just natural conversations, useful English, and plenty of laughs. Whether you're studying for school, preparing for travel, or just want to improve your everyday English, this podcast is for you.

Helen and Hannah also tease the next episode, where Hannah will interview Helen about her personal journey in learning and teaching English—a story full of challenges, funny moments, and inspiration.
