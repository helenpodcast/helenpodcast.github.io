In this episode of the PoducateMe podcast, the format is flipped as co-host Helen interviews her co-host Hannah about her personal journey in language learning and teaching. Hannah shares how she unexpectedly got into tutoring during university to support herself financially, which later led her to discover a passion for teaching. After graduating during a recession, she moved to South Korea to teach English and connect with her Korean heritage. What was supposed to be a one-year stint turned into an eight-year adventure, during which she gained deep professional experience and significantly improved her Korean language skills through immersion and personal relationships. Hannah discusses the skills she developed teaching young learners, her growing love for ESL instruction, and how that ultimately led her to pursue teaching as a long-term career upon returning to Canada. The episode ends with a lighthearted quick-fire round and words of encouragement for those considering teaching abroad.
