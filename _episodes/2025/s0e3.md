In this fun and fast-paced episode of PoducateMe, Hannah and Helen geek out over one of their shared passions: amusement parks. The conversation kicks off with Helen’s recent visit to Canada’s Wonderland, and spirals (in the best way) into stories of terrifying drops, nostalgic rides, and the physics of roller coasters. Along the way, the hosts compare theme park culture in North America and Ukraine, debate the merits of wooden coasters, and share their most memorable experiences at Disney World, Disneyland, and Universal Studios—including interactive wands, stormtrooper arrests, and emotional moments in the Harry Potter world.

The episode offers a mix of humor, travel memories, and cultural insights, all through the lens of thrill rides and childhood dreams. Whether you’re a roller coaster lover or someone who keeps their feet on the ground, you’ll enjoy the energy, laughs, and surprises packed into this ride of an episode.
