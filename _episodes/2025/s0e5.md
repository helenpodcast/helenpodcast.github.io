In this episode of PoducateMe, the hosts dive into a playful yet thought-provoking scenario: what if a zombie apocalypse really happened?
They kick things off by recalling how the topic first came up - a casual hallway conversation that turned into a running joke about survival strategies. From there, the discussion unfolds into a mix of humor, imagination, and surprising honesty.
Where would you want to be? Both agree: home feels safest. Though IKEA or Costco might sound tempting with supplies and space, the chaos would be overwhelming.
- First moves: One host admits she’d probably surrender right away rather than fight a hopeless battle. The other is determined to regroup with friends, grab essentials, and survive as long as possible.
- What to take: Priorities included food, water, warm clothes, a first-aid kit, and anything that could double as a weapon.
- Group roles: Teacher instincts shine through. Both see themselves as organizers or “behind-the-scenes leaders” rather than front-line fighters. One even embraces the idea of being the lookout, always on watch for approaching danger.
- Morality and survival: They wrestle with tough questions - what happens if a loved one is bitten? Is surviving always worth the cost? Adaptability, they agree, is humanity’s strongest trait.
- Pop culture tie-ins: Naturally, the conversation turns to favorite zombie media, from The Last of Us and Train to Busan to classics like 28 Days Later and comedies like Shaun of the Dead.

The tone stays lighthearted, but the episode underscores something deeper: these “what if” scenarios reveal a lot about how we see ourselves, our values, and the roles we’d play under pressure.
