In this episode of PoducateMe, we dive into the fascinating world of untranslatable words — those quirky, emotional, or culturally rooted expressions from Korean, Ukrainian, and Russian that simply don’t exist in English. From “ass hands” and “spill dude” to Korean “anju,” “chimaek,” and the deeply felt “han” and “chong,” we explore the richness of language and what gets lost (or hilariously improvised) in translation. A fun, heartfelt, and often ridiculous journey through clumsiness, alcohol culture, emotional depth, and why sweet cherries deserve their own word. Tune in, laugh with us, and share your own untranslatable gems.
