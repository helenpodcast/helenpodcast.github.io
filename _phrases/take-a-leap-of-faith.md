title: Take a leap of faith
featured: false
examples:
  - "Moving to a new country was a leap of faith for her."
  - "Starting my own business was scary, but I had to take a leap of faith."
---

To take a leap of faith means to do something even though you're unsure of the outcome, trusting that things will work out. It involves courage and belief, especially when there's no guarantee of success.
