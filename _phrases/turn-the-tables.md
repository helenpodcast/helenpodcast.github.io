title: Turn the tables
featured: false
examples:
  - "He was losing at first, but then he turned the tables and won the match."
  - "The company turned the tables on its competitors with a bold new strategy."
---

To turn the tables means to reverse a situation so that you now have the advantage instead of the other person. It's often used when someone goes from losing or being in a weak position to gaining control or winning.
