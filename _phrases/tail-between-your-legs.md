title: Tail between your legs

  - "After losing the argument, he walked away with his tail between his legs."
  - "They came back with their tail between their legs after the failed mission."
---

To have your tail between your legs means to feel embarrassed, ashamed, or defeated, especially after a failure or mistake. The phrase comes from the way a dog acts when it's scared or has done something wrong.
