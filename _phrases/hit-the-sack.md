title: Hit the sack
featured: false
examples:
  - "I'm exhausted, I'm going to hit the sack early tonight."
  - "After the long flight, all he wanted to do was hit the sack."
---

This idiom means to go to bed. 'Sack' is a slang term for bed, and the phrase has been used in American English since the 1940s.
